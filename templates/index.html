How it works

What is this?
A free tool that generates realistic-looking fake profiles (name, phone, email, username…) for testing and development. Nothing here is a real person.
Are the profiles unique?
Yes — every generated name, phone number and username is tracked so you never get the same one twice.
What is AI Name?
Instead of picking from a list, an AI model invents a natural-sounding name guided by real name patterns for the selected country.
Is my history saved?
Your last 10 generated profiles are kept in this browser only (via a random visitor ID). Clear your browser data to erase it.
Is there a limit?
Yes — 20 requests per minute per visitor, to keep the service fair for everyone.
